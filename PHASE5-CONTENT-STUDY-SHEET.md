## Use
Open the repository through GitHub Pages or a local web server so `data/questions.json` can load. The study sheet is available from the main menu.

## Interview practice note
Treat model answers as scaffolding, not scripts. Keep them truthful, avoid claiming clinical practice during the interval, and answer in your own natural voice.
